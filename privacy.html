Privacy Policy

Solo Cleaner · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Solo Cleaner is a mobile app that helps independent cleaners price jobs, send quotes and track what they earn. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who is responsible

Solo Cleaner is provided by Burak Erkent, an individual developer based in Türkiye ("we", "us"). For anything about your data, write to burak.erkent27@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

InformationExamples
AccountAn anonymous account ID created when you first open the app. If you save your account with an email address (or, where offered, sign in with Apple or Google), that email address and the sign-in identifier from that provider.
Business detailsYour name, business name, country and, if you add them, your phone number, business email and logo.
Pricing profileYour hourly rate, room times, services, add-ons and monthly goal.
Your work recordsQuotes and quote PDFs, jobs, time worked, travel time, payments you record (amount, date, method) and expenses.
Your clients' detailsWhat you type about a client: name and, optionally, phone number, address and notes.
PurchasesWhich plan you have, whether it is active and when it renews or ends. We never receive your card or bank details; payment is handled by Apple or Google.
Usage and diagnosticsWhich screens and features are used, app version, device model, operating system, language, and crash and error reports. We do not work out your location from your IP address.

The app does not access your contacts, microphone or location. If you add a logo, the app opens your photo library so you can choose it, and only the image you pick is uploaded.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: save your pricing profile, build quotes and PDFs, track jobs and show your earnings.
  • To provide Pro: check that a subscription or purchase is active.
  • To fix problems and improve the app: understand which features are used and where the app fails.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not sell your information, show adverts, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

4. Your clients' details

You decide which client details to enter, and you are responsible for having the right to store and use them for your business. We keep them only to provide the app to you and do not contact your clients or use their details for anything else.

6. Legal bases (UK and EEA)

  • Contract: to provide the app and Pro features you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the app secure, fix errors and understand how it is used.
  • Consent: where the law requires it; you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information by law.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, pricing, clients, quotes, jobs, expenses and stored files are deleted from our live systems. Copies in our providers' backups are removed on their normal cycle. Usage and error records held by PostHog and Sentry are tied to a random account ID, not to your name or email; they are not removed by deleting your account and are kept for the limited period those services apply. Email us if you want them removed sooner. Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are kept as those services require.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app under Settings > Your account > Delete account, or by emailing us (see how to ask without the app). Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
  • Analytics: you can switch usage analytics off in Settings > Share usage data.
  • Access, correction, export, objection: email us and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the UK or EEA you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk). If you live in California, you have the right to know, delete and correct your personal information and not to be discriminated against for using those rights; we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can only read and change its own records. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

10. Children

Solo Cleaner is a business tool for adults. It is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
