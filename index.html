Support

Questions, problems or feedback: write to burak.erkent27@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

  • Cancel a subscription: open your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it.
  • Restore a purchase: in the app, go to Settings > Restore purchases.
  • Move to a new phone: save your account first (Settings > Your account), then choose "I already have an account" on the new phone.
  • Delete your account: Settings > Your account > Delete account. See below if you no longer have the app.

Delete your account and data

In the app: Settings > Your account > Delete account. This removes your profile, pricing, clients, quotes, jobs, expenses, logo and stored quote PDFs straight away.

Without the app: email burak.erkent27@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Solo Cleaner account", from the email address your account is saved with (or tell us the address, or that you saved it with Apple or Google). We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

An account that was never saved exists only on the phone it was created on, so we cannot match a request to it. Delete it from inside the app.

What is kept: purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat, as those services require, and usage and error records tied to a random account ID for a limited period. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
